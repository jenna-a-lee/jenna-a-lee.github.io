The Young Women's Conference is held at Princeton University and hosted by the Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory’s (PPPL). In 2023, the conference hosted nearly 800 middle and high school girls, providing talks and activities with the goal of educating the public on STEM research and making the STEM pipeline more diverse. 

## Activities included:
* Ocean acidification -- hands-on demonstration showing how adding $CO_2$ (produced from a baking soda & vinegar reaction) changes the pH of water; visualized using a colorful pH indicator
* Cloud in a bottle -- hands-on demonstration of how air pressure and temperature contribute to cloud formation
