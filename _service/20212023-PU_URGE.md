URGE aimed to deepen the Geoscience community’s knowledge of the effects of racism on the participation and retention of People of Color in the discipline through a structured curriculum. 
More info is available at [URGEoscience.org](https://urgeoscience.org/)

## Responsibilities included:
* Organizing the participation of the Princeton University Dept. of Geoscience and Atmospheric & Oceanic Sciences in the NSF Funded URGE Program -- This included managing the 45 member GEO/AOS Pod, including faculty, graduate students, undergraduate students, and staff during the initial 16-week program
* Leading a 10+ member “podlet” during weekly meetings to discuss live interviews with DEIA leaders and to contribute to biweekly deliverables. Worked with other podlet leaders to combine and summarize 8 deliverables for submission to URGE organizers
* Co-leading an 8-person group to continue URGE efforts following the initial program. This work focused on researching the departmental procedure for hiring faculty and reviewing graduate student applications and comparing it to similar programs at other universities and recommendations from a literature review. Findings were then summarized and changes were recommended for the procedure of prospective faculty and graduate student interviews
