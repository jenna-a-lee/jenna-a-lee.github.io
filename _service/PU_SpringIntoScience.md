Spring into Science is an annual event open to 4th-10th graders and their families held at Princeton University. The event hosts over 800 students from a wide variety of backgrounds and provides hands-on introduction to science and engineering through demonstrations, tabletop activities, research talks and more.

## Activities included:
* Ocean acidification -- hands-on demonstration showing how adding $CO_2$ (produced from a baking soda & vinegar reaction) changes the pH of water; visualized using a colorful pH indicator
* Cloud in a bottle -- hands-on demonstration of how air pressure and temperature contribute to cloud formation
* Phytoplankton microscopy -- an introduction to microscopy using fixed phytoplankton slides and an explanation of the importance of phytoplankton as primary producers
